Foundational course covering microcontroller architecture, peripherals, C/C++ firmware development, and debugging practices used in real-world embedded products.

## Course Outline

- Module 1: Microcontroller Architecture and Peripherals
- Module 2: C/C++ Firmware Fundamentals
- Module 3: Debugging and Toolchains
- Module 4: Hands-on Capstone Project

## Classroom Training Hours
**40 hours** of instructor-led classroom training, combined with supervised hands-on lab work.
