## Course Outline

- Module 1: Machine Learning Fundamentals
- Module 2: Data Preparation and Model Training
- Module 3: Applied Engineering Case Studies
- Module 4: Model Evaluation and Deployment Project

## Classroom Training Hours
**30 hours** of instructor-led classroom training, combined with supervised hands-on lab work.
