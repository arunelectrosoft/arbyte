Covers digital design fundamentals, HDL programming (VHDL/Verilog), and FPGA-based development for high-performance and hardware-accelerated applications.

## Course Outline

- Module 1: Digital Design Fundamentals
- Module 2: HDL Programming (VHDL/Verilog)
- Module 3: FPGA Toolchains and Synthesis
- Module 4: Hardware-Accelerated Design Project

## Classroom Training Hours
**36 hours** of instructor-led classroom training, combined with supervised hands-on lab work.
